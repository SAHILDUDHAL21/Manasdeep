About Anugraha Foundation

A Pune NGO built on inclusion, empowerment, and compassion.

Established in 2013, Anugraha Foundation is a Pune-based not-for-profit NGO supporting persons with disabilities (PWD) — including individuals with Autism, Cerebral Palsy, Intellectual Disabilities, and Down Syndrome. We deliver physiotherapy, occupational therapy, special education, rehabilitation, parent counselling, and livelihood training that empower people to lead more independent and fulfilling lives.

Our focus is engaging persons with intellectual and developmental disabilities in meaningful activities that bring structure and positivity to their day, while also supporting their families. Through consistent rehabilitation work, we strengthen Activities of Daily Living (ADL), behaviour management, and social skills — enabling greater independence, dignity, and community participation.

We are a registered not-for-profit entity and have 80G and FCRA registration wherever applicable.

Featured Initiative

POSH Consulting

We help organisations across Pune and beyond build safe, respectful, and legally compliant workplaces through our Prevention of Sexual Harassment (POSH) consulting services. From policy drafting to Internal Committee (IC) setup, awareness training, and annual compliance reporting — our team partners with you to create workplaces where every employee feels safe and respected.

  • POSH policy drafting and Internal Committee setup
  • Employee awareness workshops and manager training
  • Investigation support and annual compliance reporting
  • Tailored programmes for corporates, NGOs, and institutions
